SITE INFORMATION

Privacy information.

A plain-language summary of the information that may be processed when you use this website.

Enquiry form

If you submit the website enquiry form, the information you choose to provide—such as your name, email address, project details and message—is sent through FormSubmit so it can be delivered to Eyebyte at eyebyte@gmail.com. FormSubmit is a third-party service and processes submissions under its own terms and privacy information.

Site visit counter

The footer counter uses CounterAPI.com to maintain a shared visit count. Requests to the counter service may be processed by that provider along with ordinary technical request information. Eyebyte uses the returned total to display the visit reference in the footer.

Embedded and external services

The site may load embedded YouTube videos, Google Fonts, and external social or event websites. Those services may receive technical information from your browser when their resources load or when you follow a link. Their own privacy policies govern their handling of that information.

How to contact Eyebyte

For questions about a website enquiry or this notice, contact eyebyte@gmail.com.
