Live visuals are a performance discipline.

World-class visual performance that responds to the music, the crowd, the screen and the moment—not just a playlist on a wall.

Eyebyte brings live video artistry into the heart of the show. Content selection, real-time effects, timing and visual direction are shaped around the music and the energy in the room, turning screens into an active part of the stage experience.

Since 1995, Eyebyte has worked where creative ambition meets live-event reality. Thousands of events and millions of entertained people have informed a practical, instinctive approach to show-day performance: read the room, respect the music, and make the visual system feel like it belongs.

From club environments to multi-stage festivals and bespoke brand activations, the performance approach can be built around the size, shape and intention of the event. Planning happens before doors; the performance responds when the room is live.

Common questions.

Clear answers to the practical questions that help a show move forward.

What is live VJ performance?

A VJ performs and mixes visuals in real time, shaping imagery, effects and timing to the music and the atmosphere in the room.

Can visuals be timecoded?

Yes. Timecoded moments can be built into a show where precision is required, alongside live performance and real-time response.

Does Eyebyte work outside Sydney?

Eyebyte works across Australia, including festival, club, stage and event environments.
