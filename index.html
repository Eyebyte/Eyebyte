04 / Eyebyte hardware

Peak Image Quality.
Zero Shortcuts.

Beyond the boundaries of traditional displays. With latest-generation modular LED technology, we create custom screen formats conventional flat walls simply can’t match. From bespoke LED setups and projection to precision camera and control gear—all owned, managed and delivered in-house to uncompromising standards.

Our modular LED system combines P2.9 pixel pitch with 5,000-nit brightness and a 7,680 Hz refresh rate. Accurate colour, IP65 protection on both the front and rear, and a 170° × 170° viewing angle complete a high-performance display system built for demanding live Australian environments.
