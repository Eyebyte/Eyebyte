=========================================================
Audit date: 5 November 2026

CURRENT SITE STRUCTURE
- index.html is the single public marketing site. All services, work, hardware, contact, social and privacy navigation uses in-page anchors.
- screen-builder.html is the only separate interactive page. It opens in a new tab and returns to the homepage hardware section.
- The former live-vj-performance.html, modular-led-screens.html, event-visual-content.html and privacy.html pages have been removed.
- 404.html remains as the static host's not-found handler; it links back to homepage anchors and Screen Builder.
- Privacy information is disclosed in an expandable footer section on the homepage.

KNOWN LIVE-ONLY CHECKS
