Design the spatial experience.

Custom screen formats, true flexible curved panels and modular systems designed around the stage—not the other way around.

Beyond the boundaries of traditional displays. With latest-generation modular LED technology, Eyebyte creates custom screen formats conventional flat walls simply can’t match. From bespoke LED setups and projection to precision camera and control gear, the aim is to make the technology serve the creative idea.

The modular LED system combines P2.9 pixel pitch, 5,000-nit brightness and a 7,680 Hz refresh rate. It is IP65 rated on both the front and rear, with accurate colour and a 170° × 170° viewing angle.

With more than 110 m² of system area, true flexible curved panels and dedicated hard- and soft-90° panels, screen formats can follow the architecture of the stage. Magnetic modules and power, a variety of rigging and ground-support options, and an extensive 4K control system make the setup highly adaptable.
