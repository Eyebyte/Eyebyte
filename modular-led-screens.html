Design the spatial experience.

Custom screen formats, true flexible curved panels and modular systems designed around the stage—not the other way around.

Beyond the boundaries of traditional displays. With latest-generation modular LED technology, Eyebyte creates custom screen formats conventional flat walls simply can’t match. From bespoke LED setups and projection to precision camera and control gear, the aim is to make the technology serve the creative idea.

Anchoring the technology lineup is a modular LED screen system designed for exceptional resolution, vibrant brightness, colour accuracy, high refresh rates and strong contrast. Image quality matters at the back of a festival field and at close audience sightlines, so resolution comes first.

True flexible curved panels enable forms that bend with the visual design. Modular configurations can shape a backdrop, create unusual formats or integrate screens into the architecture of a stage. Hardware, creative direction and technical delivery are coordinated in-house to keep the result coherent.

Common questions.

Clear answers to the practical questions that help a show move forward.

Can modular LED screens curve?

The flexible panel system supports curved screen configurations, subject to the chosen panel and design requirements.

Can the screen be designed for a specific stage?

Yes. The screen format should follow the space, stage design, event purpose and viewing conditions.

Can Eyebyte also provide visuals for the screens?

Yes. Eyebyte combines hardware with live performance and bespoke content, so screen design and visual content can be planned together.
