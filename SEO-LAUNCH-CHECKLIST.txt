EYEBYTE SEO & PERFORMANCE — LAUNCH CHECKLIST
============================================

BUILT INTO THIS SITE PACKAGE
- Dedicated service pages for Live VJ Performance, Custom Modular LED Screens, and Bespoke Event Visuals.
- Unique page titles, concise meta descriptions, canonical URLs and Open Graph/Twitter preview metadata.
- Organization and Service structured data, plus breadcrumbs on service pages.
- Internal links from the homepage service cards and service-to-service cross-links.
- XML sitemap and robots.txt.
- Branded favicon, Apple touch icon, web manifest, and 1200 × 630 social preview images.
- Semantic heading structure, image alternative text, keyboard skip link, and responsive service pages.
- Variable-font loading and smaller external image fallback requests.
- Privacy information describing current third-party integrations.

AFTER UPLOADING TO HTTPS HOSTING
1. Open the live homepage and every service URL on real phones and desktops. Confirm images, local videos, social links and the contact form all work.
2. Verify domain ownership in Google Search Console. Submit https://eyebyte.tv/sitemap.xml and inspect the homepage and each service URL.
3. Verify the site in Bing Webmaster Tools and submit the sitemap there too.
4. Run PageSpeed Insights/Lighthouse on the deployed URLs. Good Core Web Vitals targets: LCP ≤ 2.5 s, INP < 200 ms, CLS < 0.1. These are targets, not guarantees of search ranking.
5. Test Open Graph previews with a link-preview debugger after social metadata has gone live. The crawler must be able to fetch the PNG preview URLs.
6. If eligible, complete the Google Business Profile with accurate service areas, category and recent genuine project images; do not publish a private residential address if it is not a customer-facing location.
7. Ask festivals, venues, production partners and collaborators to link to the relevant Eyebyte service/case-study page where it is natural and accurate.
8. Add real project case studies over time: event name, role, venue/stage challenge, solution, images/video, and outcomes that can be substantiated. This gives search engines and prospective clients more specific material than a single homepage.

IMPORTANT DEPENDENCIES / CURRENT ASSET AUDIT
- The media files and supplied project logos listed in ASSET-AUDIT.txt are referenced by the site but were not present in the working folder when this package was rebuilt.
- YouTube background iframes are not fully unbranded, and playback can vary by browser/embedding policy. Replacing them with the original MP4 exports is the most reliable way to achieve clean, uninterrupted background video; provide hero.mp4 and hardware-feature.mp4 for that switch.
- FormSubmit must be activated/tested on the live site. CounterAPI is an external dependency for the shared footer counter.
- Search metadata does not guarantee indexing or rankings. Google needs to crawl the live HTTPS site; monitor results in Search Console over time.
