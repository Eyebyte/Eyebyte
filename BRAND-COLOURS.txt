EYEBYTE — BURNISHED COPPER & CHAMPAGNE
====================================

Primary background:       #111213  Near-black charcoal
Surface / panels:         #1C1A19  Warm graphite
Raised surface:           #25211F  Warm charcoal
Primary text / logo:      #F5F2EB  Soft white
Secondary text:           #B9B0A6  Warm silver
Deep oxide accent:        #9E482C  Secondary / depth accent
Burnished copper:         #C47B48  Primary action and interface accent
Champagne:                #D9B77E  Highlight, fine line and emphasis

Use the white Eyebyte wordmark unchanged. Use copper for buttons and main interaction
states, champagne for readable accent text and fine detailing, and deep oxide sparingly
for depth. Avoid using deep oxide as small text on charcoal; its contrast is too low.

Typography remains unchanged: Barlow Condensed, Manrope and DM Mono.
